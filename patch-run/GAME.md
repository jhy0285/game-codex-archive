# PATCH//RUN

- **Title:** PATCH//RUN
- **Hook:** Every 20 seconds, a permanent patch changes one arena rule; patches stack into chaos.
- **Player fantasy:** Stay mobile, turn dangerous geometry into a weapon, and survive a system rewriting itself.
- **Controls:** WASD move; mouse aim; left click shoot; Space dash; F fullscreen; R restart after death.
- **First 10 seconds:** Movement, shooting, pursuing enemies, health, and the first patch countdown are immediately readable.
- **First 30 seconds:** RICOCHET activates at 20 seconds and shots begin rebounding once from arena walls.
- **Target 3-minute experience:** Escalating survival where all three rules remain active and wall shots become large, lethal returns.
- **Core loop:** Move → aim → shoot → evade → kill → adapt to permanent patches → survive until death → restart.
- **Win condition:** Gate 1 has no terminal win; the goal is a longer, higher-scoring run.
- **Lose condition:** Player integrity reaches zero.
- **Score condition:** Each defeated enemy awards 100 points.

## Must Build

Responsive movement, aim/shoot, dash, one pursuing enemy archetype, health/damage/death, fast restart, visible countdown, and the fixed RICOCHET → GROWTH → FRIENDLY FIRE stack.

## Should Build

After Gate 1: human-tested onboarding, difficulty tuning, and a complete three-minute arc as scheduled.

## Never Build

Multiplayer, accounts, backend, inventory, campaign, dialogue, level editor, or monetization.

## Parking Lot

- Random patch selection, additional patches, enemy archetypes, and arena variants.
- **EVADE BONUS:** Reward close dash escapes with a short score multiplier.
- **PATCH CHAIN:** Consecutive kills with bounced or grown bullets build a visible trick-shot multiplier.
- **HOTFIX WINDOW:** A risky center zone appears just before installation and pays a score bonus if held until the patch lands.
- Audio-reactive effects that intensify as permanent rules stack.
